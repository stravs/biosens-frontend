RESULTS AND HYPOTHESES FOR DISCUSSION:

Conductivity sensor ok, R2 = 0,9767. Alpine karst rivers have lower conductivity than drinking waters (bottled of tap).

Fluorescence sensor ok, R2 = 0,9475. Not yet quantified.

Temperature sensor is slow. This is not problematic for intended use.

Nephelometer measurement of effluent mixtures were not consistent. Sava Dolinka_2 shows effect of artificial dam.

Spectrophotometer measurements are repeatable. Difference between clean waters (rivers, bottled), distilled water and effluent mixtures can be observed, best at 940 nm.
